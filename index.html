ONGOING RESEARCH · SJTU

SteerWAM: world–action models for robot manipulation

On SteerWAM, I am mentored by Guangyang Wu under the supervision of Prof. Xiaohong Liu. My work includes implementing trajectory guidance, training models, and conducting rollout evaluations on RoboTwin and LIBERO.

I am currently exploring the integration of a world model into SteerWAM to evaluate whether it can improve robot manipulation performance.
